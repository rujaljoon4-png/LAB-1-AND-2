This repository contains my practical learning portfolio for the Generative AI for Business course as a BBA Aviation Management student. The project documents hands-on laboratory activities focused on understanding, applying, and evaluating Artificial Intelligence in aviation and business contexts.

The portfolio explores the fundamentals of Generative AI, Traditional AI, AI tool selection, prompt usage, information verification, and human judgement. It includes activities using tools such as ChatGPT and Google Gemini, along with documentation and submission through GitHub. The labs demonstrate how AI can support aviation-management tasks such as flight-delay communication, passenger feedback summarisation, airport awareness campaigns, airline content creation, demand forecasting, suspicious-booking detection, and passenger-service activities.

A key focus of the portfolio is understanding the difference between retrieved and generated information and recognising the importance of verifying AI-generated information before using it. It also examines how Traditional AI and Generative AI can work together in practical aviation workflows while keeping human review and decision-making involved.

Each laboratory activity includes prompts, AI-generated outputs, classification exercises, comparisons, aviation examples, reflections, verification activities, and AI-usage declarations. This repository represents my academic journey in understanding how Generative AI can be responsibly applied to aviation management, business operations, communication, and decision-making.
